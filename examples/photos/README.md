# Gallery example photos

These landscape images are sample content for trying the upload and gallery UI. They are not family photos and are not imported automatically. Start Gollery, sign in at `/admin` using your local credentials, and upload the files from this directory to see the populated gallery.

All three images are from Unsplash and are used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), which permits free download and use. Attribution is not required by the license, but is included here as a courtesy. The files are resized JPEG downloads (1500px wide) of the originals.
